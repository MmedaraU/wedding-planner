# 💍 The Google Sheets Wedding Planner

Welcome! This project is a free, private, and mobile-friendly wedding planner. 

Instead of paying for a monthly subscription app, this project turns your personal Google Sheet into a beautiful, modern web app that you can access from your phone. Your data stays 100% in your control (in your Google Drive).


> **What's new in this version**
> - **Two apps, one backend.** A public wedding site (invitation, RSVP, gallery, gifts) sits beside the planner. Both talk to Google Sheets.
> - **RSVP → Guest List auto-sync.** RSVPs submitted on the public site are automatically added to or matched against the Guest List. New names land in an "RSVP (auto-added)" bucket that you review before generating invitations.
> - **Physical cards, not QR codes.** Check-in is a printed name-tick sheet. The QR scanner was removed.
> - **Two Apps Script projects.** The planner's backend (`@OnlyCurrentDoc.txt`) and the public site's backend (`=========...txt`) are deployed separately, so neither can break the other.


**What it does:**
*   **Live Countdown:** See exactly how many days are left.
*   **Savings Tracker:** Track your total saved (Base Savings + Contributions) against your dynamic goal, with a monthly target calculation.
*   **Dynamic Budget:** Automatically calculates your "Available Cash" by adding your base savings and contributions, then subtracting the deposits you've paid.
*   **Guest & RSVP List:** Track who is coming, who is pending, and who needs a hotel.
*   **Vendor Pipeline:** A fully dynamic view that merges any sheet marked as a vendor source.
*   **Task Management:** A macro timeline for planning (sorted by date) and a micro timeline for the Day-Of schedule.
*   **Day-Of Schedule:** A custom view that groups events by day (TM, Wedding, Reception) and displays the exact time beside each activity.
*   **Invitation Generator:** Generate personalized PDF invitations from Google Slides templates (Email & E-card only).
*   **Thank You Generator:** Create personalized Gmail drafts or WhatsApp click-to-chat links for gifts and cash received.
*   **Automated Reminders:** A script that emails the couple a monthly digest of upcoming tasks and their live savings progress.

*   **Public Wedding Site:** A separate invitation site (RSVP form, gift details, photo gallery, letters to the couple) with its own deployment.
*   **RSVP Sync:** Every RSVP submitted on the public site appears on the Guest List within seconds — no manual copy-paste.
*   **Review Queue:** Auto-added RSVPs are flagged and highlighted in the planner until you've set their category and invitation type.
*   **Couple Notifications:** You get an email the moment a guest RSVPs or leaves a letter.

## 🚀 1. How to Set It Up

### Step 1: Prepare the Google Sheet
Make a copy of the provided template. This sheet is your database. Ensure the following sheets exist:
*   `_app_config`: Tells the app which tabs to show, and which sheets are vendors (Column P).
*   `_app_meta`: Holds key/value data. Crucial keys: `wedding_date`, `couple_names`, `savings`, `contact_email`, `savings_goal` (optional, defaults to 30M), and the Named Range `Budget_Total_Deposits`.
*   `budget_flat`: A flat mirror of the budget (generated by the script).
*   `Day-Of Timeline`: A new sheet to track minute-by-minute events for the wedding day.

*   `RSVP`: A sheet for public RSVPs. Headers needed: `Timestamp`, `Name`, `Attending`, `Email`, `Guests`, `Requests`, and optionally `CheckedIn`. (Any other columns are tolerated but stay blank.)
*   `Letters`: A sheet for public "letters to the couple". Headers: `Timestamp`, `Name`, `Message`.
*   `Guest List`: Must contain columns named exactly `Names`, `Guest Response`, `Number to expect`, `Category`, `How to invite`, `Invited to`, `Accomodation Option`. The sync matches on `Names`; the planner reads the rest.

#### Step 2b — Public site backend

1. In the **same** Apps Script editor (**Extensions > Apps Script**), click **File > New > Script file** and name it `public`. Paste the contents of `=========...txt`.
2. Find `const EMAIL_CONFIG` and set `coupleEmail` to your real address(es) (comma-separated).
3. Click **Save**.
4. From the function dropdown, select `syncAllRsvpsToGuestList` and click **Run** once. It will say "0 RSVPs reconciled" on a fresh sheet — that's correct. It also grants the extra mail scope on first run.
5. **Deploy:** Click **Deploy > New deployment**. Select **Web app**. Execute as: **Me**. Access: **Anyone**. Copy the URL — this is your **public API URL**.
6. Both projects can live in the **same** Apps Script file, but they must be deployed separately (two "New deployment" entries).

### Step 3: Set up the Frontend (GitHub Pages)
1. Fork this repository to your GitHub account.
2. Open `config.js` and paste your Web App URL. *(Note: Because of the new login screen, do not put the API_SECRET in `config.js` anymore).*
3. Go to **Settings > Pages**, set the branch to `main`, and save.

### Step 3b — Public site

The public wedding site (invitation, RSVP, gallery, letters) is a **separate** front end. Common patterns:

- **Separate repo** — e.g. `wedding-site` — deployed to its own GitHub Pages URL (`mmedara.github.io/wedding-site`). Guests get this URL only.
- **Subfolder** — publish the public site at `/` and the planner at `/planner/`. Same repo, two entry points.

In whichever setup you choose:

1. The public site's JS needs two URLs, both from `config.js`:
   - `PUBLIC_API_URL` → the URL from Step 2b
   - (The planner URL is only used by the planner, not the public site.)
2. The public form should **not** send `API_SECRET` — guest submissions are intentionally unauthenticated so guests can submit without credentials. The public script routes by `formType` before any auth check.
3. Add `<meta name="robots" content="noindex">` and a `robots.txt` with `Disallow: /planner/` so search engines don't index your budget.

## 📱 2. How to Use the App

*   **The Sidebar:** Tap the ☰ icon to open the menu and jump between Budget, Guests, Timeline, etc.
*   **Editing:** Tap the **⋯** (three dots) next to any item to open a mini-form to change prices, dates, or notes.
*   **Refreshing:** The app saves data for 5 minutes to load fast. If you make a change in the Google Sheet and don't see it on the app, tap the **Refresh** button in the bottom left.
*   **Day-Of Schedule:** This tab uses the `Time` column to render a chronological minute-by-minute timeline. It automatically reads your `_app_meta` dates to label the days (e.g., "Traditional Marriage — Thursday, Dec 14"). 
    *   *(Note: It uses `.getDisplayValues()` on the backend to prevent timezone conversions. The time is displayed exactly as typed in the sheet, so 8:00 AM will always show as 8:00 AM regardless of the guest's timezone).*
*   **Login:** The app is protected by a password screen. It checks the password against your Apps Script backend. Once you enter it, it remembers it on your device.


### Reviewing RSVPs

When a guest RSVPs on the public site, the sync adds a row to your Guest List with **Category = `RSVP (auto-added)`**. In the planner's **Guests** view:

- A **yellow banner** at the top tells you how many rows are waiting for review.
- Tap **"Show them"** to filter the list to just those rows.
- Auto-added rows appear with an **amber tint** and a small dot next to the name.

For each row, set three things before you generate invitations:

1. **Category** — change it from `RSVP (auto-added)` to the real bucket (`Bride's Extended Family`, `Couple's Friends`, etc.).
2. **How to invite** — defaults to `Email` if they left an email. **Change it to `Physical Card`** for anyone you're mailing (or hand-delivering) an invitation, otherwise the invite generator will try to send them a PDF.
3. **Invited to** — required by `generateInvites()`. Rows with this blank are silently skipped by the generator (this is a safety net — you cannot accidentally invite an unreviewed guest).

Once all three are set, the amber tint disappears. Rows stay visible in the regular list.

### Check-in on the day

Physical invitation cards are the mechanism — no QR codes, no scanner.

**Before the wedding:** From the planner, open **Guests** and sort by name. Print the view (or export the sheet). Two copies: one for the ceremony door, one for the reception. A third as spare.

**On the day:** Ushers tick off each guest as they arrive. If someone shows up who isn't on the list (plus-one, forgotten cousin), ushers write their name on the blank rows at the bottom of the printout and wave them through. **Never block a guest at a wedding** — sort it out after.

**The legacy `CheckIn` handler** still exists in `=========...txt` for anyone who wants to wire up their own UI later. It expects a `CheckedIn` column in the RSVP sheet and a form-encoded POST with `formType=CheckIn`. Nothing currently calls it, so it's harmless dead code. If you do reactivate it, remember it only checks the `RSVP` sheet — not `Guest List`.

## 📊 3. The "Golden Rules" & Admin Quirks

The app is smart, but it relies on the Google Sheet being formatted a certain way. Please follow these rules to avoid breaking the app:

1.  **Never insert rows above Row 1.** Always insert new rows below the headers.
2.  **The Budget Mirror Quirk:** The app uses a flat mirror (`budget_flat`) to stay fast. If you manually **insert or delete an entire row** in the main Budget sheet, you *must* click **⚙️ Workbook Tools > Refresh budget_flat from Budget**. 
    *   *(Note: A weekly trigger is installed for Year 1. You will switch this to a Daily trigger in Year 2).*
3.  **Available Cash Calculation:** The app calculates Available Cash using `Savings + Cash Contributions - Total Deposits`. It reads the deposits from a Named Range called `Budget_Total_Deposits`. 
4.  **Adding Vendors:** To add a new vendor category, create a new sheet, add it to the `_app_config` sheet, and put a label in Column P (`vendorKind`).
5.  **The `_app_config` Tab Alignment (Day-Of Timeline):** Because the Day-Of Timeline skips a few columns (like `statusField`), it is highly sensitive to tabs. Ensure the `_app_config` row has exactly this layout:
    `Day-Of Timeline` | `Day-Of Schedule` | `1` | `2` | `1` | `TRUE` | `11` | *(blank)* | `data` | *(blank)* | `Done` | `Activity` | *(blank)* | `Time` | `Activity,Time` | *(blank)* | `renderDayOfSchedule` | `FALSE`


6.  **The RSVP Sync Rules.** The sync is **forgiving and safe by design**, but these are the constraints:
    - **Match on `Names`.** The sync looks up each RSVP against the `Names` column in `Guest List`, ignoring titles (Mr, Mrs, Pastor), the word "family", and punctuation. If the RSVP says `"Ike Okpara"` and Guest List says `"The Okparas"`, they will **not** match — the RSVP lands in the `RSVP (auto-added)` bucket. That's the correct fallback; add the missing link manually.
    - **The sync only writes two columns on an existing row:** `Guest Response` and `Number to expect` (only when attending). It **never** touches `Category`, `How to invite`, `Invited to`, `Tier`, `Invited by`, or any other column. Your curation is safe.
    - **New names are appended, not merged.** Auto-added rows sit at the bottom of Guest List until you review them.
    - **Re-running is safe.** The `syncAllRsvpsToGuestList` function (from Step 2b) is idempotent — running it twice produces the same result as running it once. Use it after bulk-editing the `RSVP` sheet by hand, or if you've restored a backup.
    - **Sync failures never block the guest.** If the sync throws for any reason, the RSVP row still writes and the guest still gets their confirmation email. Failures show up in **Executions** (⏱ icon) inside Apps Script.
7.  **`_app_config` Guest List row, Column O (`quickAdd`).** Set this to:
    `Names,Category,Number to expect,How to invite,Invited to,Guest Response`
    Without this, guests added via **+ Add guest** in the planner won't have an `Invited to` value, which means `generateInvites()` will silently skip them.
8.  **`Accomodation Option` column.** Kept for manual use. The RSVP form no longer captures accommodation (form field removed), so the sync doesn't write to it. Set it to `Hotel` by hand for out-of-towners staying in the partner hotel. The planner's Hotel stat and Hotel pill in the Guests view read this column.

## 💌 4. Automations (Invitations & Thank Yous)

### Invitation Generation
1. Create Google Slide templates with `<<Name>>` on the actual slide canvas.
2. Run **⚙️ Workbook Tools > Generate ALL Guest Invitations**. 
3. The script skips physical cards and anyone who already has `Generated` in Column M. You can delete the word "Generated" to force a re-run.

4. **The generator also skips any guest whose `Invited to` is blank.** This is the safety net that prevents "RSVP (auto-added)" rows from getting a PDF before you've reviewed them. If a guest you *expect* to invite didn't get a PDF, check their `Invited to` cell first.


### Thank You Generation
1. Ensure `Gifts Received` and `Cash Contributions` have `Phone`, `Email`, `Thank You Method`, and `WA Link` columns.
2. Run **⚙️ Workbook Tools > Generate Thank Yous**.
3. **Email:** Creates a draft in your Gmail.
4. **WhatsApp:** Generates a click-to-chat link in the `WA Link` column.
5. **In Person:** Skipped automatically.
6. Rows are marked as `DRAFT CREATED` so you can run it multiple times without duplicating messages.

### Automated Reminders
The `sendCountdownReminders` script runs daily and sends an email on the 1st of every month (or if a task is overdue). 
*   **Financial Goal:** It dynamically calculates your total saved (Base Savings + Cash Contributions) against your `savings_goal` (defaults to ₦30M if not set in `_app_meta`).
*   **Monthly Target:** It divides the remaining balance by the months left to tell you exactly how much to save each month.
*   **Action Items:** It pulls upcoming and overdue tasks from the Planning Schedule.


### RSVP Notifications & Sync

The public script (`=========...txt`) runs three things on **every RSVP submission**, in this order, guarded so that a failure in one never affects the others:

1. **Write the RSVP row** to the `RSVP` sheet (all form fields).
2. **Sync to Guest List** — matches by name (upsert). See Golden Rule #6.
3. **Email the couple** a notification with the guest's name, attendance, email, party size, and any requests.
4. **Email the guest** a confirmation (only if `Attending = Yes`), with the wedding date, party size, and a reminder to bring their invitation card.

For **Letters**, the script writes to the `Letters` sheet and emails the couple a copy of the message. Guest gets nothing.

**No cron, no trigger.** All four steps fire synchronously inside the single `doPost` request. By the time the guest sees "✓ Received" on the public site, Guest List is already updated and emails are queued.

**If something seems wrong:**
- Guest says they didn't get a confirmation → check **Executions** in the public site's Apps Script. Look for `Guest email failed:` in the logs — usually a bounced address.
- Couple says they didn't get a notification → check the `coupleEmail` value in `EMAIL_CONFIG` at the top of `=========...txt`. Comma-separated, no spaces.
- Sync didn't add a guest to Guest List → check the `RSVP` sheet has the row; then run `syncAllRsvpsToGuestList` from the editor. If it still fails, look at the last row's `Name` vs the Guest List `Names` column — likely a spelling mismatch that `normalizeName` couldn't bridge.
